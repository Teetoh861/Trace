Five grades

Every finding says how certain it is, not just what it is.

✓

Confirmed by available records

Official records corroborate the document provided.

○

No adverse record identified

Nothing conflicting in the sources searched. Not a guarantee of title.

!

Discrepancy identified

Records conflict with your document or with each other.

?

FAQ

Questions people ask first.

How long does a review take?

Between 3 and 6 working days depending on the checks you choose. The quote shows the estimate before you pay, and you can follow every step in the app.

What documents do I need?

Whatever the seller gave you: a Certificate of Occupancy, deed of assignment, survey plan, Governor's consent or receipts. You can start without any and we will tell you what is missing.

What if you find a discrepancy?

The report says exactly what conflicts, shows the evidence and recommends the next step, such as a re-survey or a legal review. You decide what to do.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. Pay in full, pay 60% now and 40% before the report is released, or pay by bank transfer.

Is this legal advice?

No. TRACE reports state what the records show in the sources searched. They are not legal advice or a guarantee of title.

Which areas do you cover?

This demo uses Lagos examples. A real launch would start in one state and expand as registry access grows.
